A random effects randomizer for Curiosity: A Cat Climbing Game

Includes a mod menu to toggle settings, which are:
- Randomize Collectables
- Random Effects (With a slider to adjust the delay timer)
- Meow gives a speed boost (2x while the meow audio plays, effects ground and swim speed)

There are currectly 18 random effects that can happen

These always have a chance at playing:
- Dash unlocked/locked
- Double Jump unlocked/locked
- Spawn a bouncy drum directly on the player
- Spawn a yarn ball
- Spawn confetti
- Spawn a cannon aimed at the player
- Spawn a hand that tries to grab the player
- Inverted colors

Temporary effects (lasts until next effect, cannot happen twice in a row):
- Locked camera
- Reversed camera controls
- Upside-down camera
- Rotating camera
- Constant jump (only on ground)
- Low gravity
- Frozen in place
- Infinite jumps
- Player becomes invisible

Rarer effects:
- Launched in a random direction
