A random effects randomizer for Curiosity: A Cat Climbing Game

List of effects currently in:

These always have a chance at playing:
- Dash unlocked/locked
- Double Jump unlocked/locked
- Bouncy drum spawns on the player
- Yarn ball drops on the player
- Inverted colors

Temporary effects (lasts until next effect):
- Locked camera
- Reversed camera controls
- Upside-down camera
- Constant jump (only on ground)
- Low gravity
- Frozen in place

Rarer effects:
- Launched in a random direction
